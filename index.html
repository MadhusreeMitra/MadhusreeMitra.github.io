01

Research Profile

Plant and algal biochemist specializing in metabolomics, lipidomics and mass spectrometry applied to cell walls, microalgal biorefinery and renewable resources[cite: 4]. Expertise in targeted and untargeted LC-MS/GC-MS workflows, multivariate data analysis and protocol development for cuticle, suberin and wood lipid characterization[cite: 5]. Research spans microalgal omega-3 production, biofuel processes and biomass valorisation to recent work on xylan modification, lignin-carbohydrate linkages, and wood recalcitrance[cite: 6]. Extensive experience in mentoring, student supervision and scientific leadership in postdoc associations[cite: 7].
