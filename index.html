Madhusree Mitra

Metabolomics & Plant Cell Wall Biology

GitHub / LinkedIn / ResearchGate / Email

I am a plant and algal biochemist specializing in metabolomics, lipidomics and mass spectrometry applied to cell walls, microalgal biorefinery and renewable resources. My expertise centers on targeted and untargeted LC-MS/GC-MS workflows, multivariate data analysis and protocol development for cuticle, suberin and wood lipid characterization. My research spans microalgal omega-3 production, biofuel processes and biomass valorisation to recent work on xylan modification, lignin-carbohydrate linkages, and wood recalcitrance. I also have extensive experience in mentoring, student supervision and scientific leadership in postdoc associations.
